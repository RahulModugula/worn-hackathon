Meet Priya

Priya is not real.

She is a fictional nurse in our synthetic demo. Not a composite, not a patient interview, not a real person. We have no nurse interviews, so we will not pretend to.

  • Evening med pass. One hand on the cart, the other in a glove.
  • Her patient is asleep and the wristband is under a blanket.
  • Every tap on a screen is a hand she does not have.

What gets in the way

Seamless Handoff

Two devices, one truth.

A colleague taps the same dose on a phone a second later. The server keeps the first write and shows who gave it. Either device can undo for 10 seconds.

No double dose. No arguing devices.

When she needs a second opinion

Photo on the glasses. Draft on the phone.

Priya captures a wound. The phone shows before and after with a draft note for clinician review, and sends it to the attending.

A draft for review. It does not measure, stage or diagnose, and the nurse decides.
