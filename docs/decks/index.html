Same product, same honest status, three angles. Pick by room. Each deck has speaker notes (N), fullscreen (F), arrow-key navigation and print-to-PDF at 16:9. Offline, single file.

A. Priya's shift

9 slides · open deck

Best fit

Story-minded and generalist reviewers, a mixed room, anyone who has to remember one thing tomorrow. Best on a cold stage audience.

Slides 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 on screen, with the live demo between the product slide and the closing slides. Timing from docs/demo-script.md: problem 0:00-0:20, one sentence 0:20-0:40, demo 0:40-2:25, who pays 2:25-2:50, ask 2:50-3:00.

90-second table cut

Slides 1, 2, 3, then demo at the table (scan, done, conflict), then slide 9 ask. Flagged slides: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9. Hand the phone to the reviewer for the Done tap.

Fictional character must be labelled out loud or it reads as fake evidence. Only one number, so a business-minded reviewer may find the money thin (answer from PITCH.md). Demo carries the story: if the demo fails, the deck loses its payoff.
